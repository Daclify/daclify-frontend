# Delegation of Authority Policy

**DAO:** [DAO_NAME]  
**Version:** [DOCUMENT_VERSION]  
**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how governance authority may be delegated to administrators, committees, project reviewers, treasury operators and other authorized persons.

## 2. Delegation

Every delegation shall identify:

- The authorized person or role.
- The specific powers granted.
- Applicable financial and operational limits.
- The start and expiry of authority.
- Reporting and accountability requirements.
- Revocation and emergency procedures.

## 3. Limits

Delegates may not exceed their approved authority, circumvent voting requirements or grant themselves additional powers without valid authorization.

## 4. External Representation

Only persons with legally valid authority may sign contracts, make binding representations or act for a legal entity.

Technical access or possession of a signing key does not automatically establish legal authority.

## 5. Delegation Records

Delegations and material changes shall be documented in the DAO's approved records.

## 6. Revocation

Delegated powers may be revoked according to the approved governance rules and any binding contractual or legal requirements.

## 7. Automated Agents

Automated systems or AI agents may assist with authorized tasks only within explicitly approved permissions and safeguards.

An agent's technical ability to prepare or execute an operation does not itself constitute permission to bind the DAO.

## 8. Accountability

Delegates remain accountable for compliance with their responsibilities and applicable law.
