# Conflict of Interest Policy

**DAO:** [DAO_NAME]  
**Version:** [DOCUMENT_VERSION]  
**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

## 1. Purpose

This policy protects the integrity of decisions where personal, professional or financial interests could improperly influence governance.

## 2. Definition

A conflict of interest exists when a member's interests, relationships or obligations may materially interfere with the member's ability to act in the DAO's interests.

## 3. Disclosure

Members must disclose material actual or potential conflicts before participating in affected decisions.

Disclosures should identify the nature of the relationship, affected proposal and relevant financial or personal interest.

## 4. Managing Conflicts

The DAO's authorized governance body shall determine proportionate safeguards.

These may include independent review, additional transparency, restrictions on delegated authority or recusal.

Voting rights shall not be removed solely by this policy unless the DAO's legally valid governing rules authorize that restriction.

## 5. Related-Party Transactions

Transactions involving administrators, members or related parties must be reviewed under transparent procedures and documented with sufficient information to establish their purpose and approval.

## 6. Violations

Material nondisclosure may result in corrective action under the DAO's governance and dispute procedures.

## 7. Records

Conflict disclosures and decisions shall be retained with appropriate privacy protections.
