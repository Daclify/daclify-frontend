# DAO Dispute Resolution Policy

**DAO:** [DAO_NAME]  
**Version:** [DOCUMENT_VERSION]  
**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to internal disputes involving membership, governance procedures, conduct, delegated responsibilities and approved projects, to the extent legally permitted.

## 2. Informal Resolution

Members are encouraged to resolve disagreements through respectful discussion where appropriate and safe.

## 3. Formal Complaint

A member may submit a complaint to [DISPUTE_CONTACT], identifying the relevant facts, decision or conduct and requested outcome.

## 4. Independent Review

Complaints shall be reviewed by authorized individuals without material conflicts of interest.

Affected parties should have an appropriate opportunity to present evidence and respond.

## 5. Decision

The reviewer shall provide a reasoned outcome and any authorized corrective measures within [REVIEW_PERIOD], subject to reasonable extensions.

## 6. Appeal

An affected member may request an appeal within [APPEAL_PERIOD].

The appeal must be considered by an authorized reviewer or body not materially conflicted in the original decision.

## 7. External Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy prevents a person from exercising mandatory legal rights, reporting unlawful conduct or using legally available remedies.

## 8. Records

Dispute records shall be retained with appropriate confidentiality and access controls.
