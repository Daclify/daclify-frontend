# DAO Code of Conduct

**DAO:** [DAO_NAME]  
**Version:** [DOCUMENT_VERSION]  
**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

## 1. Purpose

[DAO_NAME] is committed to maintaining a respectful, constructive and inclusive environment for collaboration and community governance.

## 2. Expected Behavior

Members and contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, consider different perspectives, provide constructive feedback, act honestly and respect the DAO's established procedures.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following conduct is prohibited:

- Harassment, threats or intimidation.
- Discrimination or targeted abuse.
- Fraud, impersonation or deliberate deception.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.
- Deliberate disruption or manipulation of governance processes.
- Misuse of DAO assets, systems or privileges.
- Retaliation against anyone reporting concerns in good faith.

## 4. Reporting

Members may report suspected violations to [DESIGNATED_CONTACT_OR_COMMITTEE].

Reports shall be reviewed fairly and confidentially to the extent reasonably possible and permitted by law.

## 5. Enforcement

Depending on severity, corrective measures may include warnings, mediation, restrictions on privileges, suspension or termination of membership.

Members should be informed of material allegations and offered a reasonable opportunity to respond, except where immediate protective measures are necessary.

## 6. Appeals

Affected members may appeal enforcement decisions using the DAO's established dispute-resolution procedure.

## 7. Adoption

This policy takes effect following valid approval through the DAO's governance process.
