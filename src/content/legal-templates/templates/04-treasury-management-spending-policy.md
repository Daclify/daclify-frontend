# Treasury Management and Spending Policy

**DAO:** [DAO_NAME]  
**Version:** [DOCUMENT_VERSION]  
**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

## 1. Purpose

The DAO treasury shall support the organization's approved objectives while maintaining appropriate safeguards, transparency and financial accountability.

## 2. Ownership and Custody

The DAO shall identify which person or legal entity legally owns or controls each treasury asset.

Operational treasury records, smart-contract balances and legal ownership are not necessarily equivalent.

## 3. Authorized Spending

Treasury expenditure requires valid approval according to the DAO's applicable rules and any legally required authorizations.

The approval must specify the purpose, asset, recipient or recipient-selection process, and maximum amount.

## 4. Spending Controls

The DAO shall establish:

- A routine expenditure limit of [LIMIT].
- Enhanced authorization for amounts above [HIGH_VALUE_THRESHOLD].
- Required signers or approval roles of [SIGNER_POLICY].
- Emergency expenditure procedures of [EMERGENCY_POLICY].

Where technically available, treasury execution controls should enforce these restrictions.

## 5. Project Funding and Milestones

Projects may be funded through an approved budget and milestone schedule.

Milestone payments shall be subject to the specified completion evidence, review requirements and authorization process.

The DAO must not approve payment beyond available funds or the terms of valid commitments.

## 6. Contributor Payments

Scheduled contributor payments must be supported by approved agreements and funding.

Payment schedules do not replace employment, contractor, tax or other legally required documentation.

## 7. Asset Security

Authorized operators shall apply appropriate key-management, access-control, transaction-verification and recovery practices.

Private signing keys must not be published in DAO records.

Sensitive treasury permissions should be separated where feasible.

## 8. Financial Reporting

The DAO shall maintain an accessible record of approved budgets, transactions, commitments and material treasury risks.

Reporting frequency: [REPORTING_PERIOD].

## 9. Prohibited Activities

Treasury assets must not be used for unauthorized personal benefit, fraud, unlawful transactions or expenditure outside approved purposes.

## 10. Incident Management

Suspected theft, compromise or material financial irregularities must be escalated using the DAO's incident procedure.

Authorized emergency measures shall be limited, documented and reviewed.

## 11. Dissolution

Treasury disposition upon dissolution shall follow lawful obligations, applicable organizational documents and the approved Dissolution Policy.
