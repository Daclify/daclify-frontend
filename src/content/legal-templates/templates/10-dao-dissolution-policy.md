# DAO Dissolution and Asset Distribution Policy

**DAO:** [DAO_NAME]  
**Version:** [DOCUMENT_VERSION]  
**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the internal process for winding down the DAO in an orderly and accountable manner.

## 2. Dissolution Proposal

A dissolution proposal shall state the reasons for closure, proposed timetable, assets and liabilities, outstanding commitments and proposed administrator or liquidator.

## 3. Approval

Voluntary dissolution requires [DISSOLUTION_THRESHOLD] and any additional approvals required by applicable legal instruments or law.

## 4. Outstanding Obligations

Before distributing residual assets, the responsible parties must identify and appropriately address debts, taxes, contractual obligations, claims and other legally binding responsibilities.

## 5. Asset Distribution

After satisfying applicable obligations, any remaining assets shall be allocated according to [LEGAL_DISTRIBUTION_RULES].

Governance credits or membership status alone do not establish an entitlement to distributions.

## 6. Records

Final treasury reports, governance decisions and required organizational records shall be preserved according to law and the DAO's retention policy.

## 7. Technical Shutdown

Authorized operators shall arrange for appropriate closure or transfer of accounts, services, access permissions and technical infrastructure where technically and legally possible.

Blockchain contracts or historical records may remain accessible after organizational dissolution.

## 8. Final Reporting

A dissolution report shall describe completed actions, remaining unresolved matters and the disposition of assets.

## 9. Legal Requirements

Where a legal entity exists, its mandatory dissolution and liquidation procedures govern over conflicting provisions of this policy.
