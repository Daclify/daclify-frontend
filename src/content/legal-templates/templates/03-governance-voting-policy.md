# Governance and Voting Policy

**DAO:** [DAO_NAME]  
**Version:** [DOCUMENT_VERSION]  
**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes a consistent and transparent process for submitting, reviewing, voting on and implementing DAO proposals.

## 2. Proposal Eligibility

Eligible members may submit proposals according to the permissions and requirements approved by the DAO.

Proposals must include:

- A clear title and description.
- The requested decision or action.
- The expected impact.
- Any necessary budget or resource allocation.
- The proposed implementation process.
- Relevant conflicts of interest.
- Applicable voting requirements.

## 3. Proposal Categories

Proposals may be classified as:

**Ordinary:** Routine operational or community decisions.

**Financial:** Budget allocations, spending, grants or funded commitments.

**Administrative:** Appointments, permissions and delegated responsibilities.

**Constitutional:** Amendments to fundamental governance rules.

**Emergency:** Urgent protective decisions subject to enhanced reporting and subsequent review.

## 4. Voting Eligibility and Weight

Eligibility and voting weight shall be established using the DAO's approved membership and governance-credit rules.

The relevant voter snapshot or eligibility calculation method must be identified before voting begins.

Changes to membership or governance credits during a ballot shall affect that ballot only as explicitly provided by its governing rules and implemented voting mechanism.

If a proposal does not satisfy its quorum and approval rules, it shall not pass.

## 6. Vote Integrity

Members must not exploit unauthorized accounts, credentials, software defects or governance manipulation to obtain voting rights.

The DAO shall identify and correct material voting irregularities according to its dispute-resolution process and available technical safeguards.

## 7. Results and Execution

Approved results shall be recorded with the applicable proposal reference and decision outcome.

Approval does not authorize an unlawful action or override mandatory external requirements.

Implementation remains subject to technical execution rules, treasury controls and any applicable legal approval.

## 8. Cancellation and Correction

Proposals may be cancelled, corrected or resubmitted only under the DAO's established procedures.

Published results must not be silently altered. Corrections must be separately documented.

## 9. Emergency Governance

Emergency action may be taken only by explicitly authorized persons or mechanisms within their defined authority.

Actions must be limited to what is necessary to address the emergency.

Emergency decisions shall be documented and reviewed by the DAO within [EMERGENCY_REVIEW_PERIOD].

## 10. Policy Amendments

Changes to this policy require approval according to the DAO's applicable governance rules.
