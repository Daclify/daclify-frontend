# DAO Membership Agreement

**DAO:** [DAO_NAME]  
**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]  
**Version:** [DOCUMENT_VERSION]

## 1. Acceptance

By accepting membership in [DAO_NAME], the applicant agrees to comply with the DAO's adopted governance documents, lawful decisions and applicable policies, subject to any mandatory rights provided by law.

## 2. Eligibility

Members must satisfy the DAO's applicable eligibility requirements and provide accurate information where reasonably necessary to administer membership and meet legal obligations.

## 3. Member Rights

Subject to their role and permissions, members may:

- Participate in community discussions.
- Submit and review governance proposals.
- Vote where eligible.
- Access records made available to their membership category.
- Participate in approved projects and activities.
- Request clarification of governance procedures.

## 4. Member Responsibilities

Members agree to act in good faith, respect fellow participants, protect confidential information, disclose relevant conflicts of interest and refrain from deliberately manipulating governance processes.

## 5. Governance Credits

Governance credits, where used, represent voting or governance weight according to the DAO's configured rules.

Unless a separate legally valid instrument expressly provides otherwise, governance credits do not represent shares, equity, ownership, cash balances, transferable property rights or a promise of financial return.

## 6. Membership Changes

Membership admission, role changes, suspension and termination shall follow the DAO's adopted policies.

Before adverse action, members should receive notice and a reasonable opportunity to respond, except where urgent protective measures are justified.

## 7. Contributions and Compensation

Membership alone does not create a right to compensation.

Paid work requires separate authorization and agreement specifying the relevant deliverables, conditions and payments.

## 8. Privacy and Information

Members acknowledge that authorized governance activity may be recorded in electronic systems or on public blockchain infrastructure.

Public blockchain records may be difficult or impossible to modify or erase.

Personal data shall be processed according to the applicable privacy notices and law.

## 9. Withdrawal

Members may request to withdraw subject to legally binding commitments, outstanding obligations and the DAO's membership procedures.

Withdrawal does not automatically reverse prior governance actions or remove immutable records.

## 10. Acceptance Record

Each member’s Daclify member identifier, exact accepted document version, acceptance time and signed action are recorded separately in the associated Daclify acceptance record. Where separate legal execution is required, retain that evidence with the agreement.
