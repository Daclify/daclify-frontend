## Article 1. Establishment and Purpose

1.1. [DAO_NAME] (the "DAO") is a collectively governed organization established to pursue its stated mission through transparent participation, shared decision-making and accountable management of resources.

1.2. The DAO's mission is [DAO_MISSION].

1.3. The DAO may organize projects, manage community resources, approve expenditures, appoint contributors, maintain documents and undertake other activities consistent with its mission and applicable law.

1.4. This Charter defines the DAO's internal governance arrangements. It does not, by itself, incorporate a legal entity or establish limited liability.

## Article 2. Governance Principles

The DAO shall operate according to the following principles:

- Transparent, verifiable decision-making.
- Fair participation according to established membership rights.
- Responsible management of shared resources.
- Accountability for delegated powers.
- Protection of confidential information where appropriate.
- Compliance with applicable laws and binding obligations.

## Article 3. Membership

3.1. Membership shall be granted according to the DAO's Membership Policy and recorded in the DAO's approved membership system.

3.2. Members may submit proposals, vote, access records, undertake assigned responsibilities and participate in projects according to their assigned permissions.

3.3. Membership shall not automatically create ownership interests, employment status, profit-sharing rights or an entitlement to treasury assets.

3.4. Membership suspension or termination must follow the DAO's approved procedures.

## Article 4. Governance and Voting

4.1. Decisions shall be submitted and considered through the DAO's recognized governance process.

4.2. Each proposal must specify its intended action, voting eligibility, duration and applicable approval requirements.

4.3. Voting power shall be determined by the DAO's configured governance rules.

4.4. Unless otherwise established by an approved governing instrument, ordinary proposals require:

- A quorum of [QUORUM_PERCENTAGE] of eligible voting power.
- Approval meeting the [APPROVAL_THRESHOLD] requirement, with abstentions treated as specified in the Voting Policy.

4.5. Constitutional amendments, dissolution and other protected decisions may require enhanced approval thresholds.

4.6. Decisions shall be recorded through the designated governance system, including Daclify where applicable.

## Article 5. Governance Authority

5.1. The DAO may establish administrators, committees, delegates and other operational roles.

5.2. Delegated authority must be limited to the responsibilities explicitly assigned.

5.3. No administrator or delegate may represent themselves as having authority to bind a separate legal entity without appropriate authorization.

5.4. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed before participation in affected decisions.

## Article 6. Treasury and Resources

6.1. DAO-managed assets shall be used exclusively for approved purposes and lawful obligations.

6.2. Treasury expenditures require authorization according to the applicable governance and treasury policies.

6.3. Treasury activity and spending decisions shall be documented to support accountability and review.

6.4. Members have no automatic claim to DAO treasury assets.

## Article 7. Contributors and Projects

7.1. The DAO may authorize funded projects, milestones, grants and contributor arrangements.

7.2. Project proposals shall identify objectives, responsibilities, deliverables, budgets and approval requirements.

7.3. Payment shall be subject to the terms of the relevant agreement, approved funding and applicable law.

## Article 8. Records and Transparency

8.1. Governance decisions, policies and approved financial activities shall be documented.

8.2. The DAO may designate records as public, member-restricted or confidential.

8.3. Where records contain personal data or legally protected information, access and retention shall be managed under applicable privacy rules.

## Article 9. Amendments

9.1. Any eligible member may propose an amendment according to the Governance Policy.

9.2. Amendments must identify the affected provisions and provide the replacement language.

9.3. Approval requires [AMENDMENT_THRESHOLD] and any additional legal approvals applicable to the DAO.

9.4. Approved amendments shall be versioned and preserved.

## Article 10. Dissolution

10.1. Dissolution may occur following a valid governance decision or another legally binding event.

10.2. Existing debts, obligations and applicable legal requirements must be addressed before any remaining assets are distributed.

10.3. Remaining assets shall be handled according to the DAO's Dissolution Policy and applicable law.

## Article 11. Legal Status and Precedence

11.1. The DAO's legal status is [LEGAL_STATUS].

11.2. Where a registered legal entity is associated with the DAO, its legally binding constitutional or operating instruments prevail over inconsistent provisions of this Charter.

11.3. Applicable mandatory law prevails over all DAO governance documents.

## Article 12. Adoption

This Charter becomes effective when approved using the DAO's valid adoption procedure and, where required, formally executed by authorized parties.
