# Governance Amendment and Document Control Policy

**Version:** [DOCUMENT_VERSION]

## 1. Purpose

This policy provides controlled, transparent amendment procedures for the DAO's governance documents.

## 2. Amendment Proposal

A proposed amendment must include the affected document, existing provision, proposed replacement, rationale and effective date.

## 3. Approval

Approval shall follow the requirements of the affected document and any superior governing instrument.

A lower-level policy cannot amend a higher-level governing document merely through an ordinary vote.

## 4. Document Hierarchy

The DAO's document hierarchy shall be:

1. Mandatory applicable law.
2. Legally binding formation and operating instruments, where applicable.
3. DAO Constitution or Charter.
4. Governance and financial policies.
5. Operational procedures.

Any conflict shall be resolved according to applicable law and the legally effective governing instruments.

## 5. Version Control

Every adopted version shall include a version identifier, effective date, approval reference and record of substantive changes.

Superseded versions shall remain available where lawful and technically feasible.

## 6. Publication

Approved amendments shall be published to the authorized audience using the DAO's document management process.

## 7. Emergency Changes

Emergency restrictions may be imposed only within existing legal and delegated authority and shall be subject to applicable review or ratification requirements.
