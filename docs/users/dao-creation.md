# Saved DAO orders and claim links

**Copy order link** saves the address of your order so your own account can resume it. The address is also shown in a read-only field for manual copying. It is not an invitation and does not let another account claim the DAO. The old “this order” link pointed back to the order already open on screen.

If you reviewed a free shared setup and then decide it is for someone else, leave it uncreated. An eligible Daclify DAO executive can open **Prepare this setup for someone else** on the saved order and use the existing invitation approval flow. The link copies the immutable saved settings into a separate seven-day invitation. The first person to confirm it with their Daclify account creates the DAO and becomes its sole initial member, administrator and executive. The issuer does not join.

The original order remains unchanged and unused. Do not create both paths unless you intend two separate DAOs. Paid receipts and already-reserved claim orders do not offer this shortcut; it never transfers a payment, changes founding keys or rewrites an existing order. Account access/unlocking returns to the same saved order.

After a DAO has been created, a new claim link cannot transfer that existing DAO. Use its normal membership and role process to admit and appoint the recipient, then complete a separate handover. Its original creation and governance history remains recorded.
