# Work proposals and member services

Open your DAO’s **Works** module. **Work proposals** lists specific projects with contributors, milestone payments and funding status. **Member services** is an optional directory of skills and offers: a listing itself holds no funds and gives no roles. Choosing **Propose work with this member** opens the same proposal popup with the provider selected. You still agree the deliverables and payments and request funding separately.

Select **+** to open **Propose milestone work**. Choose an active member by their public profile name or wallet name; members without either use a stable member reference. Choose the published proposal document version describing the scope and deliverables. Public titles, filenames and text previews appear with versions; private documents use reference labels without exposing encrypted metadata. **Open DAO documents** opens another tab; **Refresh members and documents** brings new records into your draft. Missing documents must be published before submitting.

Each milestone has its own amount and optional due date. Enter positive **whole tokens**, such as **10 TLOS**. Amounts start empty; decimals, zero, negative values, grouped numbers and exponent notation are rejected rather than rounded. **Add milestone** creates another empty row; **Remove** keeps the remaining rows and dates together. You can add up to 16 milestones. The visible total uses exact integer arithmetic and must fit the token payment limit. Check **I checked the milestone amounts and total** before submitting. Changing the contributor, document, amount, date or row list clears that confirmation. A contribution agreement requires dated milestones and contributor consent before funding.

Active members can prepare a draft; an authorized signer in **Signing & wallets** is needed to submit. Cancel, the close button or Escape keeps the draft. A failed request also preserves it. Pending submission disables closing and editing; confirmed submission returns to Work proposals and clears the submitted draft. Changing DAO or account clears scoped drafts.

Filter loaded work by proposal/contributor text, status, contributor or contribution agreements. Clear filters restores the loaded list. **Load more Works records** adds older projects; filters apply to loaded projects, not unseen history. Cards show the proposal’s document version, contributor, status and milestone payment states.

Accepting a project reserves funds, or your DAO’s governance policy can require a funding vote. The contributor selects a published evidence document version when submitting a milestone. An independent authorized reviewer selects a review document and approves payment or requests changes. The existing consent, reviewer, funding and once-only settlement rules continue to apply. Cancellation preserves approved payment obligations.

The help icon beside **+** opens **Rules & help** for Works, retaining your DAO context. Public member names are optional display information; read failures use wallet/member references. Failed member/document reads offer retry and cannot substitute records from another DAO.
