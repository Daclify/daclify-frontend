# Mini apps

Open **Mini apps** to browse community tools. Apps run on their own hosting and members choose the permissions they grant with their Daclify account.

- The app list stays visible on this single page. Choose **Open app**, or expand **App details** for documentation, source links, expiry and reporting. “Maintainer verified” means the maintainer proved control of its HTTPS origin; it does not certify the app or grant DAO permissions.
- Expand **Connect an app** to choose a DAO. Community administrators can register the exact HTTPS origin and login callback, download the connection configuration and versioned starter, and check server readiness. Members can manage their grants and review activity receipts.
- Expand **AI connections** to use the deployment’s supported MCP setup with explicitly scoped permissions. Follow the linked connection guide for the selected client and version.

The **How mini apps work** disclosure explains registration, deployment and member consent. Existing links to `#discover`, `#connect`, `#ai-connections` and `#learn` still open the corresponding workflow. Opening or closing setup keeps unsaved work on this page; leaving or reloading the page does not save a draft.

To publish a directory entry, sign in, expand **Manage listings** and choose **List an app**. Enter its title, description and canonical HTTPS origin. Optional documentation, source and release identifiers are under **Documentation and supported releases**. Save the listing, request origin verification, publish the supplied proof at the specified URL, then complete verification. Publication requires the governing DAO’s moderation policy to be available.

Expand **Manage listings** to use **My listings**, then **App details** and **Edit listing** to maintain an entry. Existing listing IDs, verified transfers and withdrawal are under **Existing listing, transfer or withdrawal**. The successor must prove origin control before ownership changes. Changing the listing ID clears the previous origin proof.

Choose **Report this listing** to report a specific app. Governing DAO administrators can expand **Directory moderation** and select a listing, review its reports, hide it or restore a verified listing. Reports are cleared when the selected listing changes.

When directory loading fails, **Retry directory** retries the read. Empty results are shown separately from loading or errors. Changing the account or connected deployment clears listing and report context.

For protocol details and deployment-specific instructions, use the page’s links to the connection, directory, database and MCP guides.
