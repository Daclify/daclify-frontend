# Ballots and elections

Open your DAO's **Decide** module. **Ballots** contains ordinary decisions and their results. **Elections** contains nomination, candidate voting, executive elections and representative terms. Switching tabs keeps your drafts. Keyboard users can move between tabs with the arrow keys, Home and End.

The list appears directly below the tabs. Select the large **+** on the right to open **Open a ballot** in a popup. Active members can prepare a draft; an authorized signer in **Signing & wallets** is required to submit. **Cancel**, the close button or Escape closes the popup and keeps your draft. Closing is disabled while a submission is pending. An unsuccessful request keeps the draft and displays its error inside the popup; successful creation closes it and returns to the ballot list.

With **Elections** active, **+** opens **Election and term rules** for active DAO administrators. Choose representatives or executives, a seat count, an existing rules document version and nomination/term dates. Rules explain eligibility, responsibilities and how the election works; their selected version is recorded with the election. The term must start after nominations close and the saved voting period finishes. Dates show your device’s timezone. Executive elections retain their existing paired-account and governance-policy requirements.

Document choices show available public titles, filenames or text previews, with versions. Untitled and encrypted documents keep a stable reference label; the popup does not decrypt private content. **Open DAO documents** opens another tab so you can review or publish rules while keeping the draft; **Refresh documents** loads new versions. Loading failures offer retry. Missing documents or governance policy block scheduling and explain the next step.

A term’s **End this term early** disclosure lets an active administrator choose a published document explaining the reason. Its selected version is recorded with the recall. Existing financial obligations and permanent roles still follow their separate rules. The reason field appears beside the term, rather than as an unexplained identifier above the list.

Voting duration uses minutes, hours, days or weeks. The supported range is one minute to 30 days. Quorum and approval always use percentages: 50% quorum and 50.01% approval are the default thresholds. Up to two decimal places are supported; approval must be above 50%.

If the DAO has a saved governance policy, new ballots follow its duration, weight and thresholds. Administrators can edit those settings under **Settings → Governance**, subject to the existing policy rules and signing requirements. Opening a ballot cannot override the saved policy.

For a ballot with custom timing, **Choose a closing date** offers a date and time in your device's timezone. The preview identifies that timezone. Signing and submission take time, so the final recorded closing time can differ slightly from the preview. Cancelling the date choice restores the duration you entered.

Quorum describes the required eligible voting weight; approval describes the required support. Existing eligibility, frozen weight, finalization and election rules are described by the connected module's guide, opened with the **help icon** beside the plus button.
