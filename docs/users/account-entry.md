# Entering Daclify

Start at Account with one of three cards:

- **Login** opens Welcome back. Unlock a vault saved on this device or choose an already paired sign-in method. Back returns to the three cards.
- **New Account** opens New to Daclify? Choose E-Mail, Telegram, Telos Zero or Own Keys under Create with:.
- **Restore Account** opens recovery from an encrypted kit. Supply the kit, its original vault password or separate recovery code, and a new vault password, then select Restore. Cancel returns to the three cards.

Email and Telegram creation require the assisted-recovery checkbox and an available provider. Daclify holds a spare unlocking key in this mode. Telos Zero uses an Anchor proof from your existing native account and pairs a wallet-only Daclify profile; it does not restore signing or private-document keys. Add those separately in Account when needed. Own Keys creates the existing encrypted local vault and requires saving the kit and recovery credential before finishing. An existing saved vault requires replacement acknowledgment and stays intact during quick signup.

Back sits in the top-right corner of the Login and New Account cards. The screen blocks Back while an account or wallet request is pending. Returning from Telegram signup resumes the verified ticket in New Account. Your intended destination remains attached to all three journeys.

**Telos DAO** sits directly below Daclify DAO in the sidebar. It opens the separate Telos governance screen, including ballot history retained by the configured chain's contracts. My DAO remains your community list. Testnet records are testnet records; voting requires separately qualified Decide signing configuration and a native wallet approval.
