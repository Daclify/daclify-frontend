# Publish and understand a DAO ballot

Open **Decide > Ballots > Open a ballot**. Give the decision a title and a short explanation so members know what they are voting on. On supported confidential DAOs, the portal encrypts the title, explanation and custom choice labels before publication by default. Unlock your original account keys to publish or read this wording through the permitted DAO content-key grant. Missing keys, ciphertext or inconsistent content prevent voting; sign-in or a wallet enabled for approvals alone does not supply decryption keys.

Select **Publish ballot wording publicly** only after reviewing that disclosure. Public DAOs and explicitly public ballots publish their wording on chain. Existing plaintext ballots and previous public versions remain public. You can also select an exact supporting-document version; its contents can remain encrypted regardless of whether the ballot wording is public.

Choose **Approve or reject a proposal** for fixed Reject/Approve choices. Choose **Choose one option** for a poll with two to sixteen distinct choices, including a neutral two-option poll. A member makes one selection; its weight follows the DAO's saved member, credit or deposited-stake policy.

Review the displayed voting weight, duration, quorum and approval threshold before opening. Saved DAO rules cannot be changed for one ballot. A unique leading poll choice must reach the same approval threshold: a plurality with less than the required approval does not pass. Ordinary ballots are advisory; funding, amendments and executive elections use their own authorized flows.

For a private ballot, select **Read private ballot** to open its wording locally. Ordinary vote choices, participation, weights, totals and results remain public; encryption of the wording does not make these votes secret. Numeric choice indices and exact protected-document references remain public technical data. An attachment opens its recorded document version and commitment, rather than substituting the latest version. If that version is unavailable, the reader explains this. An attachment link alone creates no archive-retention guarantee.

Titles are limited to 160 characters, explanations to 2,000 characters and labels to 80 characters. Public serialized metadata is limited to 4,096 UTF-8 bytes; Unicode and JSON escaping may reach that limit earlier. Private wording uses an exact encrypted-document reference and the existing upload limits. Move longer supporting material into a document.

Reload the current portal before voting on custom polls; older cached interfaces may display binary labels. Existing binary ballots keep their original meaning. Unsupported or inconsistent choice metadata prevents voting in the current interface. Current ballots use `votecommit`, which binds the vote to the contract-checked definition shown to the member. A changed definition rejects the stale vote. Legacy pre-definition ballots retain their original vote path and meaning; funding, election and dispute ballots retain their typed execution rules. See the [module capability review](module-capability-gaps.md).
